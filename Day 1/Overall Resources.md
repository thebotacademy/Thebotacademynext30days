# 10 Free AI Tools Every College Student Must Know in 2026

College assignments involve more than studying. You may need to understand difficult concepts, research a topic, prepare notes, build projects, write professionally, and create presentations.

In this video, we explore ten AI tools through practical demonstrations, using a beginner-friendly engineering topic to connect research, learning, coding, and presentation preparation.

## HOW TO USE THE PROMPTS

Start with the five ChatGPT prompts:
- Personal tutor
- Exam preparation
- Active recall quiz
- Notes and flashcards
- Study plan and weakness analysis

Then try the five Gemini prompts:
- Beginner explanation
- Analogy and its limits
- Worked example
- Visual learning plan
- Mini-project

For Perplexity, use the five company research prompts to build a research brief, compare financial periods, construct illustrative scenarios, review uncertainty, and create an evidence-based report.

For Grammarly, try the five intentionally incorrect writing samples from the video and review the suggested corrections.

## IMPORTANT NOTE

AI tools can make mistakes, generate unsupported claims, or produce code that does not work. Verify important information against original sources and test generated code.

Free features, usage limits, account eligibility, and pricing can change. Check each tool's official documentation before use.

The company and stock research segment is educational. It is not personalized investment advice, a guaranteed forecast, or a recommendation to buy or sell shares.

Use AI to learn, verify, and improve your work—not to replace your own understanding.
